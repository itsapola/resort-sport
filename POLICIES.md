# Delivery & Returns

*The part where we're plain and exact.*

## Delivery

**Complimentary standard delivery** on every order within the United States. Arrives in 3–5 business days.

**Express delivery**, $25. Arrives in 1–2 business days.

**When it ships.** Orders placed by 1 pm PT on a business day leave the same day. Everything else leaves the next business day. You'll get a tracking link as soon as it does.

**How it arrives.** In a Resort Sport Ltd. box, with the bag in its dust bag and wrapped in tissue. No plastic filler.

**Signature required.** A bag like this shouldn't be left on a doorstep, so every delivery needs a signature. If you won't be in, you can redirect it to a pickup point from the tracking link.

**Where we deliver.** All 50 states, including Alaska and Hawaii (add 2 business days). We don't ship internationally yet. Join the Time Off Club and we'll write when we do.

**Taxes.** Sales tax is calculated at checkout. There are no other charges at the door.

## Returns

**30 days from delivery** to return any bag, for any reason.

**What we ask.** Return it unused, in its original condition, with the dust bag and tags.

**Return shipping is on us.** Start a return from your order email and we'll send a prepaid label.

**Refunds** go back to your original payment method within 5 business days of the return reaching us.

**Limited Issues** follow the same 30-day policy. If one sells out while yours is on its way back, we can't hold another, but your refund is guaranteed.

**Something wrong with it?** If a bag arrives damaged or faulty, write to us within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund it, whichever you prefer.
